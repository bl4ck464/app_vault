Distribution Policy

Learn how to distribute your Android applications through APKVault.

Read the Policy
01

Security First

All apps must be scanned for malware and verified for security. We prioritize user safety above all else. Applications containing malicious code, spyware, or undisclosed tracking will be rejected immediately.

02

Open & Independent

APKVault welcomes independent developers. No corporate gatekeeping — if your app is useful, safe and well-built, it belongs here. We celebrate developer freedom and user choice.

03

Transparency

Every app listing must clearly state what permissions it requires, what data it collects (if any), and who the developer is. Users deserve to know exactly what they are installing.

04

Timely Updates

Developers are expected to maintain their apps with reasonable update cadence. Abandoned apps may be flagged and eventually removed to keep the store reliable and current.

05

User Feedback

We encourage developers to listen to user feedback and respond constructively. Apps with consistent negative reports regarding functionality or safety will be reviewed.

06

No DRM or Restrictions

Apps distributed through APKVault must not enforce DRM, root detection or other restrictive measures that limit user freedom. Users should be able to use apps on their own terms.
